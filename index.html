Study Guide

Atomic Structure

  • Atomic number = number of protons
  • Mass number = protons + neutrons
  • Electrons are negative
  • Neutrons have no charge

Bonding

  • Ionic bonds transfer electrons
  • Covalent bonds share electrons
  • Metals usually form positive ions
  • Nonmetals usually form negative ions

Acids and Bases

  • Acids have pH below 7
  • Bases have pH above 7
  • Neutral solutions have pH 7
  • Litmus indicators change color

States of Matter

  • Melting and evaporation are endothermic
  • Freezing and condensation are exothermic
  • Gas particles move fastest
  • Solids have fixed shape and volume
