Atomic Theory

Atoms contain protons, neutrons, and electrons. Atomic number equals protons. Isotopes differ in neutrons.

Periodic Table

Electronegativity increases up/right. Atomic radius increases down/left.

Bonding

Ionic bonds transfer electrons. Covalent bonds share electrons.

Stoichiometry

Use balanced equations and mole ratios for calculations.

Acids & Bases

Acids produce H+ ions. Bases produce OH− ions.

Redox

Oxidation is loss. Reduction is gain.
