Study Topics

Atomic Theory

Atoms contain protons, neutrons, and electrons. Atomic number = protons. Mass number = protons + neutrons. Isotopes have different neutrons.

Periodic Table

Groups have similar properties. Electronegativity increases right/up. Atomic radius increases left/down.

Bonding

Ionic bonds transfer electrons. Covalent bonds share electrons. Metallic bonds have a sea of mobile electrons.

Stoichiometry

Use balanced equations and mole ratios to calculate masses, moles, and particles.

Acids & Bases

Acids produce H+ ions. Bases produce OH-. pH below 7 is acidic, above 7 is basic.

Redox

Oxidation is loss of electrons. Reduction is gain of electrons. OIL RIG.

Organic Chemistry

Hydrocarbons include alkanes, alkenes, and alkynes. Functional groups determine properties.

Gases

Boyle's Law: pressure and volume are inverse. Charles's Law: temperature and volume are direct.
